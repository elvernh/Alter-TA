## Corrections for the manuscript

**[14]** The citation is correct as written (vol. 51, no. 5, pp. 561–580, May 2002). IEEE has two records for this paper: 10.1109/TC.2002.1004595 (no. 5, May 2002) and 10.1109/12.980014 (no. 1, pp. 13–32). The citation matches the first. You can add its DOI:

> W. Chen, S. Toueg, and M. K. Aguilera, "On the quality of service of failure detectors," IEEE Trans. Comput., vol. 51, no. 5, pp. 561–580, May 2002, doi: 10.1109/TC.2002.1004595.

**[17]** The manuscript gives only the IEEE Xplore URL. The full details, which the SKANIKA revisi7 version already had, are:

> H. Hanada and K. Ishibashi, "Service-Level Objective-Aware Load-Adaptive Timeout: Balancing Failure Rate and Latency in Microservices Communication," IEEE Access, vol. 13, pp. 137881–137895, 2025, doi: 10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3596118.

Minor: Crossref lists [1] as published online on 2025-12-03, in vol. 38, issue 1 (2026). Citing it as 2026 is acceptable.
